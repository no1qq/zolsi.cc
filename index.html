Internet safety, in plain language

Staying safe online is a skill. Anyone can learn it.

Zolsi.CC explains how the internet and social media actually work, what the risks really look like, and what to do when something goes wrong. No jargon, no scare stories, and nothing you need an account for.

If you remember nothing else

Three habits prevent most of the harm.

  • 01

    Slow down before you answer

    Urgency is the tool. A real bank, a real friend and a real official will all wait five minutes while you check.

  • 02

    Keep private things private

    Your address, your school, your money and your body are not content. Nothing online needs them to work.

  • 03

    Tell someone early

    Almost every situation is smaller and easier to fix on day one than on day thirty. Secrecy is what makes it grow.

Common questions

Before you go further

Is Zolsi.CC only for children?

No. The child safety pages are the heart of the site, but the same skills protect everyone. A grandparent avoiding a bank scam and a fourteen year old avoiding a fake profile are using the same three habits. Every page is written to be read out loud and understood by anyone.

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. Nothing here assumes you know what a browser, a VPN or two factor authentication is. Where a technical word is unavoidable, it is explained the first time it appears. If a page loses you, that is a fault in the page, not in you.

Does this site track me?

No. Zolsi.CC has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. The only thing stored on your device is your chosen text size, kept in your own browser so the site remembers it next time. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Can I report something to Zolsi.CC?

No, and it is important to be clear about that. Zolsi.CC is a guide, not a reporting service, and nobody here can act on a report. The get help page lists the organisations that can, including anonymous ones, and explains which to use for what.

Who writes this?

Zolsi.CC is an independent, non-commercial project. It sells nothing and takes no advertising. Where advice comes from a child protection organisation, that organisation is named and linked so you can read the original. See about Zolsi.CC.
